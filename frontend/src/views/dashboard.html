About Us

We specialize in toll interoperability between different highway systems, providing seamless communication and toll data analysis. Our system supports the management of toll data, enabling the automatic calculation of financial settlements between toll operators, ensuring fairness and transparency across different networks. We are committed to enhancing traffic management systems and ensuring accurate and efficient tolling operations. We also offer detailed statistical data to help our partners optimize operations and improve decision-making.

Toll Stations and Overview

Our system interfaces with several toll stations, including Aodos, Egnatia, Attiki Odos, Moreas, and others. Each station is connected to our platform, which ensures that all toll transactions are processed efficiently and accurately. Below are the toll stations integrated into our system:

  • Aodos
  • Egnatia
  • Attiki Odos
  • Moreas
  • Kentriki Greece Toll
  • Ioannina Toll
  • Thessaloniki Toll
